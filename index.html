    Featured: The Journey Accelerator

    A deterministic donor cohorting and lifecycle automation framework I architected in Marketing Cloud for UNICEF pledge and cash donors.

    The problem

    Markets needed the right lifecycle messages to reach new pledge and cash donors, and a trustworthy way to prove those messages worked. A control group is only useful if nobody leaks in or out of it.

    The key decision

    I separated permanent cohort assignment from the daily donor attribute sync. Each donor is placed once, deterministically from their ContactID, about 10% into Control and 90% into New. When their pledge or donation data changes, their cohort does not.
